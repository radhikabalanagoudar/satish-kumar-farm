# D. Satish Kumar — Family Farm & Agricultural Supply Website

A production-quality, fast, stable, and responsive web application built for the **D. Satish Kumar Family Farm** and its agricultural supply chain.

## 🌿 Overview

This website communicates the complete agricultural journey:
**LAND → CULTIVATION → CARE → GROWTH → HARVEST → SUPPLY → FUTURE**

Built with performance, authentic storytelling, and stability as core priorities:
- **Fast First Paint & Sub-second Loading**: Minimal JS footprint, no bulky animation frameworks or heavy video backgrounds.
- **Resilient Layout**: Graceful fallback system for every image; guaranteed zero-crash rendering via React Error Boundary.
- **Family Authenticity**: Reflects five years of genuine land stewardship without invented figures or generic templates.
- **Exclusive Owner Profile**: The authentic photograph of **D. Satish Kumar** is showcased exclusively in a tasteful signature section at the bottom of the pages immediately before the footer.

## 🚜 Farm Features & Structure

1. **Home Page (`/`)**:
   - High-contrast, lightweight hero with warm farm photography and dark overlay.
   - Farm introduction with 5-year milestone context.
   - Featured crop highlights.
   - 8-stage visual supply chain preview.
   - Seasonal calendar preview.
   - Long-term timber investment section.
   - Dedicated stewardship team overview.
   - Responsive gallery preview.
   - Future vision trajectory.
   - Signature owner profile & deep forest footer.

2. **Our Story (`/story`)**:
   - "Five Years of Growing with Purpose"
   - Core values: Family Stewardship, Agricultural Patience, Soil Biology, and Future Value.
   - Interactive 6-phase developmental timeline with scroll-reveal transitions.

3. **Crops Showcase (`/crops`)**:
   - **Fruits**: Pomegranate, Dragon Fruit, Mango, Lemon, Papaya, Nerala Kayi / Jamun.
   - **Vegetables**: Brinjal / Eggplant, Tomato.
   - **Timber Trees**: Long-term agricultural investment progression:
     *Sapling → Growing Tree → Mature Tree → Timber Logs → Handcrafted Furniture*.
   - Interactive crop modal displaying cultivation notes, seasonal rhythm, and local Kannada names.

4. **Supply Chain (`/supply-chain`)**:
   - Interactive 8-stage pathway:
     *01 Farm → 02 Cultivation → 03 Farm Care → 04 Growth → 05 Harvest → 06 Sorting & Handling → 07 Supply → 08 Market / Customer*.
   - Clean SVG icons and handling principles.

5. **Seasonal Calendar (`/seasons`)**:
   - Interactive 12-month calendar (JAN – DEC).
   - Monthly farm activities, relevant crops, and harvest timing.

6. **The People Behind Every Harvest (`/people`)**:
   - Resident caretaker couple living on the farm.
   - Resident operations and irrigation specialist.
   - Seasonal agricultural workforce for peak harvests, pruning, and composting.

7. **Farm Gallery (`/gallery`)**:
   - Responsive masonry grid with category filters (Fruits, Vegetables, Timber & Trees, Landscape, Farm Life).
   - Lightweight modal lightbox viewer.

8. **Future Vision (`/future`)**:
   - "Growing Today. Building Tomorrow."
   - 5-step trajectory: *TODAY → GROWTH → HARVEST → SUPPLY → FUTURE*.
   - 6 strategic pillars including soil biology, precision drip, and timber compounding.

9. **Contact Page (`/contact`)**:
   - Client-side form validation.
   - Purpose-driven enquiry selection.
   - Placeholder contacts adhering strictly to real data constraints.
   - Clean success feedback state.
